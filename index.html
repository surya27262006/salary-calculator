Salary Calculation Rules

Monthly CTC = Annual CTC ÷ 12

Basic + DA = 50% of Monthly CTC

HRA = 30% of Monthly CTC

Flexi = 20% of Monthly CTC

Gross = Basic + DA + HRA + Flexi

PF Base = Gross, maximum ₹25,000

Employee PF = 12% of PF Base

Employer PF = 12% of PF Base

1 Casual Leave per month is allowed

Extra Casual Leave = Loss of Pay
